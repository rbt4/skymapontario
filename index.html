WHY THIS IS NOT ANOTHER WEATHER APP

The map is evidence.
The answer is the product.

The Weather Network, Windy and radar apps already show forecasts and moving layers. SkyMap is narrower: it turns those weather signals into a decision for one real plan.

THE QUESTION

“Oakville, 4–6 PM. Will we get wet while we are there?”

SkyMap checks the destination and the actual visit window—not a generic city forecast or the entire day.

Set a place and time ↗
THE EVIDENCE

Measured radar is only the beginning.

SkyMap separates what is happening now from what may happen later, then compares radar motion, official futurecast, ensemble support, surface drizzle and the broader cloud front.

See the evidence together ↗
THE DECISION

Arrival, peak, easing and confidence—in plain language.

Instead of leaving you to interpret the map, SkyMap states whether to plan for rain and creates a large portrait image or short GIF that is easy to read in WhatsApp.

Get the answer ↗
